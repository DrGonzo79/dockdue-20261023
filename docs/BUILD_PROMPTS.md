# Phased build prompts

## Phase 1 — observed workflow
“Shadow five small-fleet dispatchers processing one unpaid detention claim each. Produce a policy-field schema, evidence checklist, rejection taxonomy, and manual runbook. Do not automate messages.”

## Phase 2 — secure claim ledger
“Add tenant auth, Postgres claims, encrypted object storage, signed upload URLs, evidence provenance, immutable audit events, retention/deletion controls, and tests for tenant isolation and duplicate submissions.”

## Phase 3 — extraction assistant
“Extract candidate free time, rate, increments, timestamps, and broker instructions from rate confirmations/BOLs. Show source spans and confidence. Require human confirmation; measure field-level precision against a reviewed set.”

## Phase 4 — approved follow-up
“Add authorization records, idempotent day 0/7/14/30 jobs, human approval, broker reply ingestion, opt-out, bounce handling, and an operations queue. No autonomous escalation.”

## Phase 5 — prove economics
“Instrument eligible dollars, submitted dollars, accepted dollars, recovered dollars, review minutes, days to payment, rejection reasons, partner CAC, gross margin, and cohort retention. Gate new integrations on proven recovery yield.”
